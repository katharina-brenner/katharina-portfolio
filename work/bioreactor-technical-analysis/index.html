  2. 02Simulate

    Represent time-dependent behavior with published modeling approaches.

  3. 03Predict

    Translate modeled behavior into a comparable yield outcome.

    Compare candidate operating points by brute-force optimization.

  5. 05Extend

    Prepare the workflow for richer kinetics, data, and future CFD coupling.
