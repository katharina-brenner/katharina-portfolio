Scope and results

Problem → model → findings → use.

Compare process configurations, inputs, utility loads, and bottlenecks.

01 / Problem

Problem: fragmented scale-up data.

Media, seed expansion, sterilization, downstream operations, cleaning, cooling, timing, and waste were modeled separately.

Need: one facility-wide model with explicit data gaps.

02 / Model

Model: one flowsheet.

The model links literature-grounded assumptions across media preparation, sequential suspension-cell expansion, a 20,000 L production reactor, clarification, washing, extrusion, packaging, and supporting utilities.

Output: mass and energy balances plus scenario comparison.

03 / Findings

Findings: facility bottlenecks.

Depleted medium, cleaning-in-place effluents, cooling demand, and the trade-off between heat sterilization and sterile filtration emerge as critical facility-level considerations.

Key systems: wastewater, CIP, cooling, and sterilization.

04 / Decision

Use: prioritize validation.

Compare configurations, identify missing data, and rank validation work.

Deliverable: open model with full data export.
