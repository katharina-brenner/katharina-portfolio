02 / Point of view

Industrial bioengineering is a whole-system challenge.

It needs people who can move between unit operations, facility infrastructure, process models, and the decisions they inform.

That is the space I work in: building transparent models of industrial production systems, identifying what really limits scale, and translating engineering evidence into tools people can use.
