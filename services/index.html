Common questions

A clear starting point.

What types of processes can you model?+

I focus on industrial and bioprocess systems where unit operations, equipment, utilities, time, economics, and uncertainty interact. The model can cover one critical decision or connect an entire facility.

Can a project begin with incomplete data?+

Yes. A useful first model makes missing data explicit, separates evidence from assumptions, and shows which uncertainty is important enough to resolve next.

What can custom laboratory software include?+

Typical components include guided experiment workflows, structured data capture, calculations, dashboards, visualizations, models, simulators, exports, and integrations with existing data or instruments.

What should I send to start a conversation?+

Share what you are building, the decision that is currently blocked, the scale you are working at, and any data, diagrams, spreadsheets, or assumptions already available.
